Welcome to spineless!

1

Open spineless

Find the spineless app on your iPhone and tap it. If you’re asked to sign in, follow the instructions on the screen.

2

Take a picture of your bookshelves

Tap the camera button. Point your phone at a shelf of books and take a picture, with the book spines facing you.

Tip: Good lighting helps. Try to hold your phone straight in front of the books so their titles are easy to read. You can add more shelves later.
3

Let spineless identify your books

spineless starts recognizing the books in your photo. You can search right away, but results may be limited at first. Identification can take up to 15 minutes to finish.

Good to know: You don’t need to stare at the screen while it works. Check back a little later.
4

Find a book

Use Search to look for a book by title, author, or subject. Tap a result to see more about the book and where it is on your bookshelf.

Browse your library: Switch to List view to see the books spineless has found. You can also explore books by genre. Add another bookshelf whenever you like.
